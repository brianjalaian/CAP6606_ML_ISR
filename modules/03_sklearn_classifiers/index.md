# Module 3: ML Classifiers with Scikit-Learn

A guided tour of the workhorse classifiers in scikit-learn — how each one draws its decision boundary, what its hyperparameters control, and when to reach for it in practice.

## What You'll Learn

- Logistic regression and probabilistic classification
- Support vector machines, maximum margin, and kernels for nonlinear problems
- Decision trees and how impurity measures drive splits
- K-nearest neighbors as a lazy, instance-based learner
- Regularization and the bias–variance tradeoff across model families
