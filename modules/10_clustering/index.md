# Module 10: Clustering

Our first dive into unsupervised learning: finding structure in data that has no labels. This module builds clustering from the ground up — how to group similar samples, how to choose the number of clusters, and how to reason about cluster quality.

## What You'll Learn

- k-means and k-means++ centroid initialization
- The elbow method and silhouette analysis for choosing k
- Hard vs. soft (fuzzy) clustering
- Hierarchical clustering and dendrograms
- Density-based clustering with DBSCAN for arbitrary-shaped clusters
