# Module 13: Convolutional Neural Networks

Deep learning for images. This module builds convolutional networks from the convolution operation up, and trains real image classifiers in PyTorch.

## What You'll Learn

- The convolution operation: filters, padding, stride, and feature maps
- Pooling, weight sharing, and why CNNs suit spatial data
- CNN architectures: stacking conv/pool/fully-connected layers
- Regularization with dropout and data augmentation
- Training an image classifier in PyTorch end-to-end
